# Resume Principles (ATS-Friendly & Recruiter-Optimized)

## Your Resume Should Be

- Easily **skimmable** by:
  - Recruiters
  - Hiring managers
  - Interviewers
- Easily **parsable** by an **Applicant Tracking System (ATS)**

---

## Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS)

- ATS is used to:
  - Scan resumes
  - Extract structured information
  - Match keywords with job descriptions
- Formatting should be:
  - Simple
  - Standardized
  - Text-based (no complex layouts)

---

## ATS Myths (Debunked)

- ATS does **not automatically reject** resumes randomly
- Fancy design does **not improve chances**
- Keywords matter, but **context matters more**
- Humans still review resumes after ATS filtering
- Over-optimization for ATS can hurt readability

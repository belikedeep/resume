# Work Experience Section Guidelines

- This section should be named **Work Experience** or simply **Experience**
- Do not use:
  - "Professional Experience"
  - "Selected Experience"
  - "Employment History"
  - "Employment"
  - "Job History"

---

## What to Include

- Only include **PAID work experience** in this section
- Research experience can also be included
- Clearly indicate:
  - Internships as internships
  - Contract positions as contract positions

---

## Ordering

- Order positions and bullet points by:
  - Relevancy to the job you're interested in, OR
  - Impressiveness (put your best work first)

---

## Tailoring & Context

- Tailor your resume for each application to match the job description
- Show that you can do the job
- Add context:
  - What you did
  - How you solved problems
- Simply listing tools or buzzwords is not enough

---

## Differentiation

- Do not just list job duties
- Focus on:
  - Accomplishments
  - Tangible metrics
  - Technical impact
- Your resume is not your job description

---

## Skills Framing

- Avoid centering your skills around software alone
- Focus on:
  - Problem-solving ability
  - Engineering thinking
  - Design and decision-making
- Tools (like CAD) should be secondary ("oh by the way")

---

## Pro Tip

- Mention doing **hand-calculation sanity checks** instead of relying only on FEA/CFD tools
- This demonstrates deeper engineering understanding

---

## Writing Style

- Use **bullet points**, not paragraphs

---

## For New Graduates

- Prioritize:
  - Fundamental engineering skills
- Deprioritize:
  - Leadership/management unless space allows

- Reality:
  - New grads are not expected to lead teams or projects
  - Focus on:
    - Mastering core engineering tasks
    - Demonstrating clear technical communication

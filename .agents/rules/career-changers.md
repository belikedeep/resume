# Career Change Resume Guidelines

- Include a brief summary (2 sentences) explaining that you’ve changed careers and your motivation
- It’s helpful for recruiters and hiring managers to have context that you have recently transitioned into software development or another field

---

## Additional Guidelines

- Link to working projects and source code
- Be concise in summarizing past work experience
- Keep your resume short
- Try to fit everything on 1 page

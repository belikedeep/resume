# Networking and Job Search Guidelines (u/emnm47's Speech)

- **Networking is critical**: Networking plays a massive role in successful job hunting when applications are online and filters are heavily used. Actively seek to connect with people rather than only sending cold online applications.
- **Utilize your immediate network**: Reach out to friends, family, ex-coworkers, peers, and professors to ask for openings. Name recognition and internal referrals are powerful advantages.
- **Follow up regularly**: Always follow up after submitting an application. People forget; following up a few times is not annoying, it is helpful.
- **Apply the structured job search process**:
  - **Step 1: Evaluate your desperation level**: Assess financial runway (savings, rent, food) and determine if temporary/part-time employment or unemployment benefits are needed.
  - **Step 2: Set a timeline**: Set a firm date (e.g., "If I haven't accepted a position by Jan 1st, I am changing x, y, and z"). Align this timeline with your desperation level.
  - **Steps 3-7: Iterate and improve**:
    - Edit and refine your resume.
    - Increase your networking efforts.
    - Revamp your LinkedIn profile.
    - Follow up on previous applications.
    - Refocus on different markets, company sizes, or geographic regions if needed.

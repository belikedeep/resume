# Resume Guidelines (Simple, Clean, Effective)

## Make Your Resume Easy to Read and Skim

- Use a **single-column layout**
- Avoid **icons, images, and graphics**
- Do **not indent sections or bullet points**. Using bullet points inherently creates indentation https://imgur.com/qBJjbth
- Use **bullet points**, not paragraphs
- Use a modern, readable font:
  - Calibri
  - Arial
  - Bitstream Charter
- Use **black font** (not thin/light)
- Minimum font size: **10.5+**
- Do **not justify text** (prevents uneven spacing)
- Maintain sufficient white space:
  - Margins: **≥ 0.4 inches**
  - Line spacing: **≥ 1.07**
- Avoid excessive:
  - Italics
  - Bold
  - ALL CAPS
- Ensure **clear section separation**
- Keep it **1 page** (unless 10+ years experience)
- Separate skills using **commas** https://imgur.com/wzvRAfB

---

## Make Your Resume More Effective

- Include **relevant technical details**
- Use **STAR / CAR / XYZ** bullet point formats
- Provide **context** for your work
- Add **keywords** relevant to the job
- Avoid unnecessary or irrelevant information
- Place **important details at the top and left**
- Think of your resume as a:
  - **30-second elevator pitch**

---

## Why These Rules Matter

- Improves readability and scanning
- Reduces distractions
- Prevents "wall of text"
- Increases usable white space
- Highlights important information quickly

---

## Keep It to One Page

### Why?

- Most resumes don’t justify multiple pages
- Extra pages often include:
  - Irrelevant content
  - Poor formatting
- Recruiters spend limited time per resume
- Concise resumes perform better

### If You Use Multiple Pages

- Only if you're targeting:
  - Senior roles
  - Staff-level positions
- Ensure all content is:
  - Relevant
  - Impactful
  - Well-structured

---

## Final Principle

> A resume should be **clear, concise, and impactful** — not dense or decorative.

# Resume Contact Information Guidelines

- If you have a security clearance, it can be listed in the contact information section at the top of your resume near your name.  
  Example: "Top Secret / SCI eligible with CI Polygraph"

- If you have a foreign sounding name, or your education/work experience doesn't make it clear you're able to work in said country you're applying to, consider listing your citizenship/work visa status near your name to be explicitly clear to the recruiter/hiring manager that you don't require visa sponsorship.  
  Example: "US Citizen", "US Permanent Resident", "Canadian Citizen", "Italian Citizen"

---

## What NOT to Include

- Don't include your physical address and ZIP code
- Don't include your location (city, ST) unless the specific job you're applying for is in that specific city
- Including a non-local location may cause implicit bias against you

---

## LinkedIn & Phone

- LinkedIn profiles are unnecessary
  - Chances are, nobody will ever click on your LinkedIn
  - If they want to find you, they can search your name on LinkedIn or Google

- Phone numbers are unnecessary
  - Interview processes don't begin with a cold call
  - Recruiters will email you if needed
  - Non-local area codes may cause implicit bias

- If you decide to list your phone number:
  - Don't preface with "Phone: ", "Cell: ", or "Mobile: "
  - If based in the US/Canada, omit the country code +1

---

## Email Guidelines

- List only **1 email address**
- Use modern providers:
  - Gmail
  - Outlook
- Do NOT use:
  - AOL
  - Hotmail
  - Yahoo
- If you've already graduated, do not use a college email unless from a prestigious school like MIT/CMU

---

## GitHub & Portfolio

- Don’t include a link to your GitHub profile if it’s empty
- Don't mask your links:
  - ❌ [email](name@gmail.com)
  - ❌ [github](github.com/username)
  - ❌ [portfolio](myportfolio.com)

- Write them in plain text:
  - name@gmail.com
  - github.com/username
  - myportfolio.com

---

## Formatting Rules

- Don't preface your email with "Email: "
- Don't preface GitHub with "GitHub: "
- Don't preface portfolio with "Portfolio: "
- Don't include https://www. in URLs
- Don't underline, italicize, or color:
  - Email
  - GitHub URL
  - Portfolio URL
